Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Who we are

Baraq is an educational startup backed by the Innovation Youth Team. This page states plainly what we collect about you right now, at this early, pre-launch stage of the project.

What we collect, and why

Right now, the only thing we collect through this website is what you type into the "waitlist" form yourself: your full name and email address. We use them for exactly one purpose: to notify you when early access to Baraq opens. There is no analytics or advertising tracking on this site.

The only other thing we store on your device is your light/dark theme preference, saved locally in your browser (localStorage) — it never reaches us at all.

Who we share your data with

No one. We don't sell your data or share it with any third party for marketing or any other purpose. Your name and email are stored only in Baraq's own database, on a server our team operates.

How to request deletion

Email us any time at barraq.ai.team@gmail.com and we'll delete your name and email from the waitlist within a few days.

What changes at public launch

This policy covers our current state only: a "coming soon" site with a simple sign-up form. Once the Baraq app itself launches and starts processing real study content (files, voice recordings, quiz results), we'll publish an expanded version of this policy covering that additional data with the same honesty — that's a hard requirement in our own internal readiness review, not just an intention.
