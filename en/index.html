FAQ

Questions every student asks before joining

Direct, honest answers — no overpromising.

When can I actually try Barraq?

We're rolling out gradually to the waitlist first. There's no fixed launch date yet, because we don't go public before end-to-end testing across every backend service is complete.

How much does Barraq cost? Is it a subscription?

Barraq uses flexible credits and packages instead of a rigid monthly subscription, so you pay based on actual usage. Exact pricing is announced when early access opens.

Does Barraq work offline?

No. Summarization, quizzes, and performance analysis all run through server-side AI processing, so Barraq needs an internet connection to work.

What data does Barraq collect, and is it private?

Right now we only collect your name and email through the waitlist form, and we never share them with a third party. Full details are in our privacy policy.

Which grade levels is Barraq designed for?

High-school and university students specifically — content, analysis, and tone are built for that stage, not a generic all-ages tool.

Is Barraq Arabic-only, or does it support English too?

The website and app experience are bilingual (Arabic/English), but Barraq is designed for Arabic-speaking students first — not translated as an afterthought from an English version.

We document our progress honestly, including what isn't finished yet — because we believe transparency builds a better, more trustworthy product over time.
